About, Privacy & Help

Privacy & How It Works

Nellie B - Chatomizer is a 100% browser-based party and conversation game. Just like our Privacy Policy at the bottom of the page, no account or sign-in is required and we never collect personal identifying information - your scores, favorites, and settings stay privately in your browser.

Experiencing Any Technical Issues?

Because the game runs directly in your browser, if it works for others and something ever looks stuck or wonky for you, try doing a hard refresh or clearing your browser cache (press Ctrl + Shift + R on Windows or Cmd + Shift + R on Mac, or clear cached images & files in your browser settings).
